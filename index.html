Questions

Good to know

Is CAW Academy an official EASA, UK CAA, GCAA or FAA product?
No. It is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by EASA, the UK CAA, the GCAA or the FAA. It is aligned with the applicable EASA, UK CAA, GCAA and FAA rules but is not an official publication and not a substitute for the official regulations or your organisation's procedures.
Which subjects and frameworks does CAW Academy cover?
Courses span four regulatory frameworks — EASA, UK CAA, UAE GCAA and FAA — across the full airworthiness lifecycle: Part-21 and 14 CFR Part 21 (initial airworthiness), Part-M and Part-CAMO (continuing-airworthiness management), Part-145 and 14 CFR Part 145 maintenance, Part 43/65 and Part 121/135 air-carrier maintenance, Airworthiness Directives, AMP development and MSG-3/MRB, the Airworthiness Review (ARC), plus Maintenance Economics, Aircraft & Engine Leasing, Fleet Reliability & Availability, Engine & LLP asset management, Offshore Registries & Article 83 bis, Aircraft Recycling, Human Factors, SMS, EWIS and Fuel Tank Safety.
How do we get CAW Academy for our team?
CAW Academy is licensed for organisations — your team gets all 75+ courses under a single agreement, with a free trial to evaluate it first. Individual in-app purchases for private learners are planned for a later stage. There are no ads, on any plan. Request a free trial.
Do learners get a certificate?
Yes. Complete a course and pass the assessment to earn a certificate with a unique number and QR code, confirmable on our public registry — audit-ready evidence of completed study. It confirms in-app study only; it is not an EASA qualification.
Is it useful for Part-66 / Part-147 exam preparation?
Yes. The recall-rated flashcards and assessments (75% pass mark) make it strong revision for Part-66 and Part-147 students sitting the EASA regulation modules, alongside working CAMO and maintenance professionals.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Once installed, every lesson, flashcard and quiz works fully offline — ideal for studying in the hangar, on the road or in the air.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone, iPad and Mac, with Android to follow — the apps are coming soon. Signed in with a licence, your progress syncs across your devices so your team picks up exactly where they left off.
Is my data private?
Yes. There are no ads or third-party trackers. Signed out, your study data stays on your device; signed in with a licence, it syncs to your account so it follows you across devices. See our Privacy Policy for the detail.
