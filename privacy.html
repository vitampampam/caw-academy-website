Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

CAW Academy (“the app”) is a study aid for airworthiness regulations across the EASA, UK CAA, UAE GCAA and FAA frameworks. We designed it to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what is and isn’t collected — in the app, and on this website (caw-academy.com), including the account and team-admin pages.

The short version

  • You can use the app without an account. If you choose to sign in to use a licence, we collect only your email address, a securely hashed password, and a record that you accepted these documents.
  • We do not use advertising, ad networks, or third-party trackers — in the app or on this website. The website sets one cookie, and only if you sign in on it.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • Your study progress stays on your device — and, if you sign in to an account, also syncs to our servers so it follows you across your devices. Your highlights and the notes you attach to them sync too, so your own study material follows you. Note text is end-to-end encrypted with a key only you hold: we store it, but we cannot read it.

Reading lessons aloud

The app can read lessons aloud. The voice runs entirely on your device. The lesson text is never sent to us or to any third party, no cloud speech service is involved, and the audio is generated as you listen — it is not recorded, stored or uploaded. Reading aloud works with no network connection at all, and nothing about what you listen to, or for how long, reaches us.

What stays on your device

Your reading progress, quiz scores, exam history, bookmarks, missed-question bank, flashcard review schedule and settings are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to us. Deleting the app removes them. You can clear them anytime in Stats → Manage & reset progress.

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress may sync across your own devices through Apple’s iCloud (key-value storage) so you can continue where you left off. This data lives in your private iCloud account governed by Apple; we never see it. You can turn this off in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

If you create a CAW Academy account, your name, learning progress and earned certificates are also stored on our servers so they sync across your devices and platforms. You can delete your account and this data at any time in the app (Profile → My account → Delete account).

If you choose to create a course-completion certificate, the name you enter is used to print it. Signed out it stays on your device and (on Apple) your iCloud; signed in it is saved to your account. You can change or delete it any time in the app.

Certificate registry. Each course-completion certificate carries a unique number issued by our server, plus a QR code, so it can be confirmed as genuine. We keep a record of issued certificates — the number, course, date and the holder name printed on it. There are two ways to verify, by design: typing a number and name only confirms whether they match (it never displays anyone's details or lets anyone look people up); scanning the QR code on a certificate you are holding shows that certificate's details (name, course, date, score), because you already have the certificate in front of you. The QR link uses a random, unguessable token. This record is deleted when you delete your account.

Information that leaves your device

Feedback. If you use the in-app feedback form, the message you type — plus the app version and your operating-system version — is sent to our feedback inbox through a relay service (Web3Forms, web3forms.com) so we can read and respond. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you and in fixing what you report. We keep feedback for as long as it is useful for that, and no longer than two years. Please don’t include sensitive personal information in feedback, and don’t use it to send health, financial or government-ID information.

Purchases. Access to the app is currently provided through organisation licences (see “Accounts and licences” below), so there are no individual in-app purchases today. If individual paid courses are offered in future, any such purchase inside the iPhone/iPad app is processed by Apple through the App Store — we never see your payment details, and Apple shares only aggregated, anonymised sales data with us. See Apple’s Privacy Policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Anonymous usage analytics (optional). If enabled, the app may send anonymous, aggregate event counts (for example: a lesson was opened, an exam was finished) to help us improve the app. These events contain no names, emails, advertising identifiers or device fingerprints, and cannot be used to identify you. If analytics is not configured, no usage data leaves your device. Because these counts cannot be linked to a person they are not personal data; where any element of it were, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding which parts of the app are used.

Accounts and licences (optional)

You only create an account if you choose to sign in to use a licence — for example an organisation seat, a redeem code, or a licence bought on our website. If you do, we process:

  • your email address (your sign-in identity and how we contact you about your access);
  • a securely hashed password (we never store your password in readable form and cannot see it); and
  • your licence entitlement — which courses your account may access, and the redeem codes or organisation linked to it; and
  • a record of your consent — the date you accepted the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy when creating the account, and which version of the two documents you were shown. We keep it so we can show that consent was given, and to tell whether the documents have changed since.

We use this only to authenticate you and unlock the content your licence grants. The legal basis is performance of our contract with you (providing the access you signed up for). If you are signed in, your learning progress, earned certificates, highlights and notes also sync to your account (see “What stays on your device” above), along with some app settings (such as your reminder time and hidden-course choices). A highlight syncs as its position and colour. The text of a note you attach to a highlight syncs as well, so your notes follow you between your devices — but it is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves the device, with a key that is unlocked by your password and never sent to us. We store only the ciphertext and a wrapped copy of that key: we cannot read your notes, and neither can anyone who obtains the stored data. The note text also stays in plain form on the device you wrote it on, so the feature can never lose a note.

One consequence of that design, stated plainly. Because the key is unlocked by your password, resetting a forgotten password makes previously synced note text unreadable — the app creates a new key at the next sign-in and notes encrypted under the old one cannot be recovered by us or by you. Notes still held in plain form on a device you are already using are unaffected. This is the necessary cost of us not holding a key of our own; the app’s export screens let you keep your own copy at any time.

Controlled documents (organisations). Where an organisation’s licence includes private controlled documents, the app has a separate reader for them. Highlights and notes made there sync in exactly the same way, under the same end-to-end encryption. The documents themselves are delivered only to accounts entitled to them and are never public.

Signed-in devices. When you sign in, we record each active device session — a device name, an app-generated device identifier and the last-used time — so you can see and remotely sign out your devices, and so we can enforce a per-account device limit. These are removed when you sign the device out or delete your account.

Course assignments (organisations). If your organisation assigns you courses, we store those assignments on your account — the course, its deadline, your completion status and assessment score — so they appear in your app and your organisation’s administrator can track the team’s progress and completion. For an assigned course, the app also reports a short progress summary — how many of its lessons you have completed, the resulting percentage, your best assessment score and when you last opened that course — together with the date you last used the app at all, so your administrator can see who may need help and whether a paid seat is being used. This is deliberately limited: your administrator sees progress on courses they assigned you and nothing about courses you study on your own, and never which lesson you are on, how you answered a question, or anything you highlighted or noted. This is part of providing the licensed service to your organisation. Deleting your account removes them.

Where it is processed. Account data is stored on servers in the European Union (Amsterdam, Netherlands), on our backend hosted by Railway (railway.com) with a PostgreSQL database. Railway is a US company acting as our processor; where any access from outside the EU occurs, it is covered by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Payments (web purchases). If you buy a licence on our website, the payment is handled by Stripe; we receive confirmation of the purchase and which licence to grant, but not your card details. See Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy. Purchases made inside the iPhone/iPad app are handled by Apple, as described above.

Account emails. We use email only to run your account: to verify your address, to send a reset link if you ask for one, and — where your organisation uses course assignments — to tell your organisation’s administrator when one of their assignments is completed or becomes overdue. These messages are delivered through an email provider acting as our processor. We send no marketing email, and there is no mailing list to be added to.

Retention. We keep account data while your account exists. Delete the account and it goes, along with your certificates and their registry entries, your device sessions, your synced progress and your assignments — we keep nothing back unless the law requires it. Feedback is kept while it is still useful for answering you and fixing what you reported. Our servers keep short-lived operational logs (request time, path, result, and the IP address the request came from) for security and troubleshooting, and routine database backups; neither is used to build a picture of you, and both age out on our hosting provider’s schedule.

Automated decisions. We do not profile you and we make no automated decision that produces a legal or similarly significant effect. Two access checks are automatic: whether your email domain allows an account to be created, and the limit on how many devices can be signed in at once. Both are simple rules with no scoring behind them, and a person will look at the outcome if you email support@caw-academy.com.

Our website (caw-academy.com)

No tracking, no advertising. The site carries no analytics, no advertising, no social-media pixels and no third-party trackers. Even the fonts are served from our own site rather than a font network, so simply reading a page sends nothing about you to anyone else. We set no cookie that needs a consent banner, which is why you are not asked to dismiss one.

The one cookie. If you sign in on the website, our API sets a single cookie, caw_refresh, to keep you signed in. It is HttpOnly (page scripts cannot read it), SameSite=Strict, sent only to the sign-in refresh and sign-out endpoints, and it expires on its own. It is strictly necessary for signing in and holds no tracking information. Signing out clears it. The team-admin portal also stores a random identifier in your browser so it can label that browser in your list of signed-in devices; clearing your browser data removes it.

Trial and demo enquiries. If you send the enquiry form, the details you type — your name, organisation, work email, role, an optional team size, the frameworks you are interested in and your message — are delivered to our sales inbox through the same relay we use for feedback (Web3Forms, web3forms.com). We use them to answer you and to set up a trial if you want one. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to an enquiry. We do not share them, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Certificate verification. The verification page sends what you type to our server to check it against the registry, and answers as described under “Certificate registry” above. You do not need an account to use it.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily study reminder, or if your organisation sets course deadlines, the app schedules local notifications on your device (one three days before a deadline, one on the morning it falls due). They are built on the device from what it already knows: no data is sent to us to deliver them, and we use no push notifications at all.

Children

The app is intended for aviation-maintenance professionals and students, and our Terms of Use set a minimum age of 16. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email support@caw-academy.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Where we hold personal data about you — which is essentially only what an account carries — you have the right to access it, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict or object to our processing of it, and to receive it in a portable form. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time; that does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

Most of these you can exercise yourself, without asking us. You can delete your account and everything on it from inside the app (Profile → My account → Delete account), clear your local study data in Stats → Manage & reset progress, and — in the iPhone, iPad and Mac app — export your own progress, or your highlights and notes, to a Markdown or PDF file you keep. For anything else — or if you would rather we did it — email support@caw-academy.com and we will act on it, and in any case within one month.

If you think we have handled your data wrongly, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to a data-protection supervisory authority: ours is the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl), and you may instead go to the authority in the EU or UK country where you live or work.

Your choices

  • Turn the daily reminder on/off in Settings → Notifications.
  • Reset local data in Stats → Manage & reset progress.
  • If you ever buy a subscription, manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
  • If you have an account, ask us to delete it and its data anytime by emailing support@caw-academy.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here with a new “last updated” date.

Who is responsible & contact

CAW Academy, operated from the Netherlands, is the party responsible for the limited data described above (the “data controller” under the GDPR). Because the app keeps your study data on your device, we hold very little. Questions about this policy, or any data request: email support@caw-academy.com or use the in-app feedback form.
