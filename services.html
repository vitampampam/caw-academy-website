Twelve service lines

Everything an aircraft and its people need on the ground and on paper — from the turnaround at the stand to crew hotels, catering and the contract that changes its owner. Each mandate is run personally by one of the two managing directors.

Ground handling

Handling supervision at airports where you have no people of your own. We appoint the agent, agree the price and stand by the aircraft through the turnaround.

Sourcing the right aircraft for one-off flights or a season of them, with operator vetting, pricing and the charter agreement handled for you.

Maintenance consultancy

Passenger services

Check-in, boarding, meet-and-assist and VIP handling, arranged and supervised so your passengers meet the same standard at every station.

Crew support

What a supervised turnaround looks like

A typical 70-minute turnaround on a mid-size jet. We hold the timeline, chase what slips, and tell you early when it will not hold — rather than after the slot is gone.

Passengers disembark10′
