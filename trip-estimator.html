This is an estimate, not a commercial offer. Nothing shown here is a quotation, a price promise or a booking. Real prices come from the operator holding the aircraft on the day and depend on the tail, its base, the dates, slots, airport and handling fees, crew duty limits and the ferry legs to and from you.

Distances are estimated track miles, not a filed flight plan: great circle plus a routing allowance that is largest on short sectors, plus departure and arrival track miles. Block time assumes no wind. Hourly rates are category averages for the European ad-hoc charter market; one-way trips include an allowance for positioning. Categories in grey cannot make the sector without more stops than are sensible, or cannot seat the party.
